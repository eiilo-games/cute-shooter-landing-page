How it plays

One thumb. The ship shoots by itself; you just decide where to be.

  1. Steer

    Drag left and right. Lasers fire nonstop.

  2. x2-6

    Pick a gate

    Gates change your number, and your number is your firepower. Shoot a bad gate and it turns good.

  3. Pop the cuties

    Anything that slips past the bottom takes a bite out of your number.

Meet the cuties

They look friendly. Most of them are.

  • Square

    Just here to wobble.

  • Shooter

    Lobs orbs at you.

  • Clinger

    Sticks to your nose. Wiggle it off.

  • Dasher

    Rattles, then charges.

  • Tank

    Slow, huge, stubborn.

  • Big Meanie

    Each one angrier than the last.

Two buttons for bad days

Big Bomb

Blows a hole in the wave. Ready every 30 seconds.

Zap Shield

Five seconds of touch-and-pop. Ready every minute.

Level up to pick upgrades, grab the wobbly mystery box if you feel lucky, and spend your coins between runs on permanent boosts. On Android, climb the leaderboards and unlock achievements with Google Play Games.

About

Cute Shooter is made by eiilo, a small independent studio. The art, sounds, music and fonts are by Kenney (CC0). The game on this page is the Android game ported to your browser: same numbers, same cuties, and no ads. The Android app is free with ads, and adds leaderboards and achievements with Google Play Games.
